I am a CS Ph.D. candidate at **Brown University** advised by [Srinath Sridhar](https://cs.brown.edu/people/ssrinath/), graduating **Dec 2026**. I build **generative world models for robot learning** — unifying video, action, and language into one representation so robots can plan, remember, and act end-to-end from pixels.

Previously: **Applied Scientist II Intern** at [Amazon Robotics](https://www.amazon.science/tag/amazon-robotics) (2024–2026), **Research Scientist Intern** at [Adobe Research](https://research.adobe.com/) (Summer 2025), and lead of the self-driving car perception stack at [RRC, IIIT-Hyderabad](https://robotics.iiit.ac.in/). B.Tech + M.Sc. (ECE Gold Medal) from IIIT-H.

I am **on the industry job market (2026)** for research roles in robotics foundation models, autonomous driving, generative modeling, and brain–robot interfaces.

### Research focus

- **Robotics foundation models** — world-action-language models, memory-aware policies, diffusion/flow-matching for action. [[UniWALM](https://ivl.cs.brown.edu/research/uniwalm/)]
- **Perception for autonomous driving** — monocular SLAM and multi-object tracking for dynamic urban scenes. [[MoM-SLAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.03528), [BirdSLAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.07613)]
- **Controllable generative priors** — steering diffusion internals for geometry, 3D, and multi-region control. [[GeoDiffuser](https://ivl.cs.brown.edu/research/geodiffuser.html) · WACV '25 Best Student Paper, [AppearancePointers](https://ivl.cs.brown.edu/research/appearance_pointers.html)]
